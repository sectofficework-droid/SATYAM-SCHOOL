## App name
SATYAM SCHOOL

## Short description (max 80 characters)
Attendance, marks, homework, fees status & notices for Satyam Stars School

## Full description (max 4000 characters)
The official student app for Satyam Stars International School, giving students and parents easy access to school information from their phone.

With the app, you can:

• View your attendance record
• Check exam marks and results
• View your fee status (view-only — no in-app payments)
• See homework assignments for your class
• Read school notices and announcements
• Check the class syllabus and chapter-wise progress
• View your class timetable
• Raise queries and get help through the in-app help desk
• View school rules and your student profile

This app is intended for students (and their parents/guardians) of Satyam Stars International School and requires a school-issued login. Accounts are provisioned by the school administration and are not open for public self-registration.

For questions or support, please contact the school office.
