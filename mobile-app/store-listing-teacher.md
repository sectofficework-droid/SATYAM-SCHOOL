## Short description (max 80 characters)

## Full description (max 4000 characters)
The official staff app for Satyam Stars International School, built for teachers to manage day-to-day classroom work from their phone.

With the Teacher App, you can:

• Mark and review student attendance for your classes
• Track your own attendance record
• View your assigned students and class lists
• Enter and manage exam marks
• Assign and track homework
• Manage personal and class tasks, including daily task lists
• Build and reuse questions from a shared question bank
• View the academic calendar and school notices
• Check the class syllabus and update chapter status for subjects you teach
• View class timetables
• Submit and track leave requests
• Raise and follow up on queries with school administration
• View school rules and your staff profile

This app is intended only for teaching staff of Satyam Stars International School and requires a school-issued login. It is not open for public registration — accounts are provisioned by the school administration.

For questions or support, please contact the school office.
