1. **Visual Fidelity to Reference PDF:**
   - Double logo headers: School crest on the top left, Shree Shree Maa Saraswati emblem on the top right.
   - Header text hierarchy:
     - "SATYAM SHIVAM SUNDARAM GROUP OF INSTITUTIONS" (Large bold maroon, serif)
     - "DAY CUM RESIDENTIAL SCHOOL"
     - "(AFFILIATED TO CBSE, DELHI)"
     - Location & contact bar: "At: Gadahadapada, Po: Kodala, Dist: Ganjam (Odisha), Pin - 761032, Mob: 9861214040, 7008137337"
     - DISE code bar: "Affiliation No. 1530325 | School Code: 16120 | DISE Code: 21193101502"
   - Pill badge: Rounded capsule with maroon fill and bold white title "TRANSFER CERTIFICATE (SCHOOL LEAVING CERTIFICATE)".
   - Metadata bar: Book No., Sl. No., Admission No., Date of Issue.
   - 18 numbered form fields with continuous baseline underline styling (`tc-fill-row`).
   - Words translation for date of birth (parsing date digits into standard hyphenated words).
   - Warning note at bottom: "N.B.:- Any change or cutting on the body of certificate makes it invalid."
   - 3-column signature block: Checked By / Office Clerk, Signature of Class Teacher, Signature of Principal (with School Seal).
2. **Asset Reliability:**
   - Saved PNG assets in `admin-panel/public/` (`tc-logo.png`, `tc-saraswati.png`).
   - Embedded Base64 data URLs in `admin-panel/src/lib/tcAssets.js` so print popups render immediately without waiting for HTTP network asset resolution.
3. **Architecture:**
   - Created centralized generator `admin-panel/src/lib/tcGenerator.js` consumed by both `/documents` (batch and single) and `/student/[id]/tc` (individual profile issuance), eliminating code duplication.

## Follow-up Iteration: Full A4 Page Height & Alignment Calibration
- **Vertical Fill Issue:** Certificate previously stopped ~65% down the page. Updated `.tc-page` to strict A4 `210mm x 297mm` with `box-sizing: border-box`, and `.tc-cert-box` / `.tc-body` to `flex: 1; justify-content: space-between;`, distributing rows 1 to 18 evenly across the full printable height.
- **Text & Underline Alignment:**
  - Standardized colon alignment across rows 1-6, 8, 9 with `.tc-col-lbl` (`width: 245px; display: inline-flex; justify-content: space-between;`).
  - Text values sit horizontally centered on solid 1.4px underlines with 1px baseline padding to prevent glyph clipping.
  - Rows 14, 15, and 18 format structured tokens with continuous underlines continuing to the right margin.
- **Preview Scaling:** Configured `TcPreview` to `width: 295, height: 418` with `scale(0.3715)` to match the full A4 canvas without vertical letterboxing.
- **Font Size Optimization:** Scaled school name to `30px`, title pill to `23px`, DISE code to `15.5px`, labels to `14.8px`, and inserted text to `15px` bold uppercase to ensure commanding legibility and balanced optical weight across the full physical A4 page.
