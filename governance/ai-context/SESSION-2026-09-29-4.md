# Session: Clear test records & resolve kiosk punchout error

## Objective
User: "we have cleared the staff attendance database still kiosk application gives punchout error as before why so?"
Follow-up: "dont close the session instead CLEAR the test records"

## Root cause
The kiosk's "punchout error" (shown as `already_in` on `face_punch_page.dart:591`) is NOT caused by `employee_attendance` data. The punch-in block is enforced by `employee_shifts` — a separate table with a partial unique index on open shifts. Clearing `employee_attendance` alone leaves `employee_shifts` intact, so staff with open shifts are still blocked.

Three RPCs enforce this server-side:
- `record_face_punch` (SUPABASE_KIOSK_SETTINGS.sql:154)
- `redeem_punch_code` (SUPABASE_PUNCH_OVERRIDE_CODE.sql:81)
- `redeem_qr_session` (SUPABASE_QR_PUNCH.sql:110)

All check: `SELECT ... FROM employee_shifts WHERE employee_id = X AND check_out_at IS NULL` — if found, returns `already_in`.

## What I did
1. **Analyzed the database** — found 19 open shifts blocking punch-in, only 4 employee_attendance rows (user had cleared it, but 4 new punches had since been added during testing).
2. **Identified test records** — Sep 17 had 15 shifts (12 already closed at 16:00 by earlier cleanup, 3 still open because punched in after shift end at 18:05/20:26/20:04). Sep 09 had 6 QR test punches from "Janki Das" within a 3-minute window.
3. **Deleted all test records** — 15 shifts on Sep 17 + 6 QR shifts on Sep 09 = 21 shifts deleted, plus corresponding employee_attendance rows on Sep 17 and Sep 09.
4. **Kept the Sep 28/29 and Sep 09 21:04 test data** — these looked like real usage (reasonable check-in/out times, not a bulk test session).
