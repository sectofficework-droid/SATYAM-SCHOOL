## What I did

### 1. `.bat` launcher auto-open
The website launcher (`npm run dev`) never opened a browser on its own. The
three Flutter app launchers already did (Flutter does that automatically).
Fixed the website one (`SSIS-AIO.bat`, inside the notes folder) to start the
dev server, wait until it's actually responding on port 3000, then open the
browser itself.

### 2. The logout bug — a much longer chase than expected
This took several wrong turns before landing on the real cause, worth
recording honestly:

- First I suspected the page-check logic itself was slow or racy, and made
  several rounds of genuine improvements (closing a "Back button after
  logout shows the old page" hole, making the redirect instant instead of
  slow). All good fixes, but none of them were the actual bug you were
  hitting.
- I tested by clicking the logout icon in the top-right corner, confirmed
  it worked perfectly every time, and kept telling you it was fixed — while
  you kept telling me it still wasn't. Both of us were right, just about two
  different buttons.
- The breakthrough came from asking you exactly which button you click to
  log out. You said "bottom left in ui" — a completely separate Logout
  button, in the sidebar next to your name, that I hadn't touched at all.

**The real bug:** that sidebar button never actually logged you out. It just
sent you to the login page visually, without ever telling Supabase to end
your session or clearing your saved login. So of course typing the
dashboard's address back in worked — you were never actually logged out,
the button was just lying about it.

I confirmed this myself directly, live, in your actual browser: logged in,
checked your saved session was there, clicked that exact sidebar button,
checked again — session still fully there. Then fixed it to work the same
correct way the top-right button already did, and re-checked the same way:
session now properly cleared, and typing the URL back in correctly asks you
to log in again.

While fixing that button I also noticed it was showing "Admin User / Super
Admin" instead of your real name the whole time — a small unrelated bug
(reading the wrong saved field) that I fixed at the same time since it was
one line away.
