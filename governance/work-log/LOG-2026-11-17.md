# LOG 2026-11-17

## What I set out to do
You asked for a way to track, per student, whether the school had called
them about collecting pending documents — their reply, the date they
promised to bring it, whether they submitted it, and whether it was then
uploaded — plus a button to call them straight from the Documents page.

## What I did
- Asked a few clarifying questions first (one tracker per student vs. per
  document, call history vs. latest state only, status stages, which phone
  number to dial) and recorded the agreed design as REQ-FEAT-023 in
  `governance/planning/TODO.md`.
- After you said "code it":
  - Created a new database table (`student_document_followups`) to store
    the follow-up info, locked down the same way the existing documents
    table is (only school staff logged into the admin panel can read/write
    it).
  - Added a new **FOLLOW-UP** column to the Student Documents spreadsheet.
    Each row shows a colored status badge (Not Called → Called → Date
    Promised → Submitted → Uploaded). Click it to open a small panel where
    you can set the status, type what they said on the call, and pick the
    date they promised to bring the document.
  - Added a phone icon next to the badge — tapping it opens your phone's
    dialer with the parent's number already filled in. If a student has two
    phone numbers on file, you'll see two small icons so you can pick which
    one to call.

## Decisions
- One follow-up record per student (not one per document type) — simpler,
  and matches how you described the request.
- Only the latest status/note/date is kept, not a history of every call.

## Then: the reminder / call-back system
Last thing you asked for today: if a promised submit date passes with
nothing handed in, there should be a reminder to call back - popping up
again and again until you either dismiss it for now or resolve it for
good. I checked a few mechanics with you first (pops up when you load any
page, not on a timer; works across the whole admin panel, not just
Documents; dismiss vs. resolve exactly as you described), then built it:

- A bell icon in the header (next to the existing notifications bell)
  that only appears when there's something to call about, with a count.
- A banner at the top of the Documents sheet, same idea.
- A popup that shows up automatically whenever you move between pages, if
  there's anything active - with each student's name, the reason, a call
  button, and a Resolve button.
- A new checkbox in the Note popover - "Also set as a reminder" - so you
  can flag a reminder on any note, not just overdue documents.

Tested it live: flagged a test reminder, watched the bell and banner show
up right away, dismissed the popup, moved to a different page and watched
it pop right back up on its own (that's the "again and again" part
working as intended), then resolved it and confirmed it was gone for good
even after fully reloading the page.

## Bug fix: note popover was broken on Student Management and Government Entry
You reported the note wasn't behaving well on those two pages and asked
where the Save button was. Found it: the popover was getting visually cut
off by those pages' own layout (a card with hidden overflow on Student
Management, a scrolling table on Government Entry) - so depending on which
student you clicked, the bottom of the popover (often including the Save
button itself) was invisible even though it was technically still there.
It only looked fine on the Documents page by coincidence of which row I'd
tested earlier.

Fixed by having the popover render itself outside that layout entirely and
position itself based on where you clicked, flipping upward automatically
if there's no room below (confirmed this near the bottom of a long table).
Tested on both pages: popover now shows fully, Save works, and clicking it
no longer risks accidentally navigating away (a related risk I found and
closed off while fixing this). Also removed the now-unused per-column
alignment option from the Documents page's note column since positioning
is automatic now.
