# LOG-2026-09-09 — Multi-shift attendance: app + admin panel wiring, merged to main

## What I set out to do
Finish the multi-shift attendance feature. The database side (a new
`employee_shifts` table plus the rules for it — block a second punch-in
until checkout, allow a new shift after) was already built and live in
Supabase from an earlier session. What was still missing: the actual apps
(kiosk, teacher app) and the admin panel didn't know about any of that yet
— they were still calling the old, single-shift code, which had actually
already stopped working on the real kiosk once the database side went live.

## What I did

**Updated the kiosk and teacher apps** to call the new backend properly:
- The kiosk's face-scan and enter-code screens now show a clear "already
  checked in since [time] — check out from your app first" message if
  someone tries to punch in while already punched in, instead of quietly
  doing nothing useful.
- The teacher app's "My Attendance" page now shows every shift someone
  worked that day (in case they clocked in and out more than once), not
  just one check-in/check-out pair.
- The admin panel's Mark Attendance screen shows the same — every shift per
  staff member for the day, not just one line.

**Found and fixed two real bugs while testing this on a virtual device
(BlueStacks), both caused by this session's own changes:**

1. **You caught this one live** — after backing out of the "enter code"
   screen a certain way, the kiosk's camera would just freeze: not
   scanning, not going back to the home screen, nothing. The camera had
   been paused for a confirmation popup and nothing ever told it to resume
   when you backed out. Fixed — it now properly resumes scanning.

2. **A permissions mistake from the earlier database session** — it turns
   out none of the three phone apps (kiosk, teacher, student) ever
   "properly" log into Supabase the way the admin panel does. They all use
   a simpler, custom login instead. Practically, that means every request
   from any of the phone apps — including the teacher app checking someone
   out — was running with lower-tier ("anon") database permissions, not the
   higher tier ("authenticated") the earlier migration had assumed. That
   mismatch silently broke the teacher app's checkout button and the new
   shift list with a permissions error, and because of a missing
   error-handling gap, the screen just spun forever instead of showing an
   error. **This is actually what you were seeing** when you asked "app ui
   shows 9:23 am check in and check out says some error why?" mid-session —
   I found the real cause right after you flagged it. Fixed by granting the
   right permissions to the right role.

**Tested the whole flow for real** against the live database (no separate
test database exists for this project) on your own staff record, with your
OK first, since there are currently no dummy/QA accounts to test with.
Confirmed: punch in → try to punch in again (correctly blocked) → check out
→ punch in again (correctly opens a new shift) — checked directly against
the database at each step, not just what the screen showed.

**Cleaned up the test data afterward as agreed — but made a mistake doing
it, caught and fixed the same session:** the cleanup accidentally deleted a
real attendance record of yours from earlier that same day (a real 9:23 AM
check-in, unrelated to my testing) along with the test rows. I'd seen that
9:23 AM entry earlier and should have double-checked whether it was real
before deleting anything for that day — I hadn't, and it turned out to be
real. I caught this immediately after and restored it from the exact values
I'd already recorded a few minutes earlier, so nothing was actually lost,
but flagging it plainly rather than glossing over it.

**Merged and pushed to `main`**, at your request ("its testing; merge to
main"). Checked first that this was safe: `main` had no other work sitting
on it that `debiprasad` didn't already have, so it was a clean,
no-conflict update — not a risky merge. Both branches are now at the same
point.
