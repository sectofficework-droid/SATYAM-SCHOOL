# LOG-2026-09-29 — Auto-close shifts at configurable shift end time

## What I set out to do

Fix a bug where staff who punch in but forget to punch out are blocked from punching in the next day. The fix: auto-close all open shifts at a configurable shift end time (default 4 PM), with a notification pushed to the teacher.

## What I did

1. **Cleared employee attendance test data** — truncated `employee_attendance` (0 rows). `sef_employee_attendance` doesn't exist in the database.

2. **Built the auto-close shifts feature:**
   - Added `shift_end_time` column to `kiosk_settings` (default 16:00)
   - New `auto_close_open_shifts` RPC — closes all open shifts past end time, pushes `teacher_alerts` notification
   - Modified `record_check_out` RPC — now also pushes a notification on manual checkout
   - Admin panel UI in Settings → Kiosk → Punch Timing

3. **Set up pg_cron** — runs `auto_close_open_shifts()` every 15 minutes inside Postgres (no external dependency, works on free tier).

4. **Split migration into two files:**
   - `SUPABASE_AUTO_CLOSE_SHIFTS.sql` — core column + RPCs (safe, no data loss)
   - `SUPABASE_PG_CRON_SCHEDULE.sql` — pg_cron setup (separate in case free tier blocks it)

5. **Committed and pushed** — `343cd62` to `origin/main`.
